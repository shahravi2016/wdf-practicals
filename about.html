About StudentHub

Purpose

StudentHub is a centralized student portal designed to provide students with easy access to academic information, campus events, notices, and essential student services. The portal aims to eliminate the need for students to check multiple sources for information.

Objectives

  • Consolidate academic and campus information in one place
  • Provide a responsive, accessible web interface
  • Enable students to view courses, attendance, and grades
  • Facilitate event discovery and registration
  • Offer a platform for student feedback
  • Support administrative functions for staff

Student Services

Academic Records

Courses, attendance, grades, and transcripts

Campus Life

Events, clubs, sports, and activities

Support Services

Counseling, career guidance, and help desk

Facilities

Library, labs, hostel, and transport info
