Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal that provides access to academic information, campus events, notices, and student services in one place.

Who can access StudentHub?

All registered students can access the portal. Public pages like Home, About, Events, Notices, FAQ, and Contact are accessible to everyone.

How do I register for an account?

Click on the "Register" link in the navigation menu and fill out the registration form with your details. You will need your student ID and valid email address.

Academic Questions

How do I view my courses?

After logging in, go to the Dashboard and click on "Courses" in the sidebar navigation to view your current courses.

Where can I check my attendance?

Attendance records are available in the "Attendance" section of your student dashboard after logging in.

How do I access exam timetables?

Exam timetables are posted in the Notices section under "Academic Notices" and are also available in your dashboard during exam periods.

Technical Questions

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password. You will receive reset instructions via email.

Is StudentHub mobile-friendly?

Yes, StudentHub is designed with responsive layouts that work on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

How do I report a technical issue?

Use the Contact form or the Feedback section in your dashboard to report technical issues to the support team.
